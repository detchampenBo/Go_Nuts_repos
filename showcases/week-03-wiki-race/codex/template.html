Data, rules & what these numbers mean

Live mode fetches Wikipedia’s parsed article body, resolves roster aliases and pins each page to the first version fetched during the race. You and BFS share that same in-memory page cache. BFS follows live edges, never the frozen graph. During the race the graph focuses on your trail and a labeled subset of outgoing choices; the buttons list every playable outgoing link. The BFS view samples crowded rings for legibility and states the full discovered count. The shortest-path computation always uses every playable link. During a live race, only fetched directed links are drawn. No result is called unreachable unless BFS finishes the reachable graph; a failed API request gives an incomplete search with a retry action.

Frozen practice is a separate, explicitly labeled mode using the 26 August 2026 course snapshot: 303 nodes and 1,784 directed edges. Practice works offline. No live edges are substituted into a practice race. Routes, visits and game state are held in memory for this tab only.

The cards use the full frozen graph: degree, closeness, harmonic, betweenness and eigenvector centrality on the undirected graph; PageRank on the directed graph with α = 0.85 and uniform dangling-node redistribution. Closeness includes the reachable-component correction; harmonic is normalized by 302; betweenness is normalized by (302 × 301)/2. Eigenvector scores use unit Euclidean norm. These are reference stats, not centralities recomputed on the live graph.

The course’s 2.67 is mean distance in the undirected giant component. It is shown only as context, not a fair target for a directed race. The practice directed reference is computed over reachable, distinct ordered pairs. Your most-visited heroes reflect your play, not necessarily high mathematical betweenness.

Article text and thumbnails come from Wikipedia contributors; text is available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Each article links to its source; live pages link to their fetched revision. Pageviews use Wikimedia’s user-agent category (“user”), all access types, for the labeled calendar month. Unavailable values are never replaced with zero.
