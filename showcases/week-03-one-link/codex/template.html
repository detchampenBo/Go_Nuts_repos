A route is not a friendship.

These stations are Wikipedia articles. A track means at least one article links to the other; we ignore direction here. The new cyan link is hypothetical.

Betweenness counts a hero’s share of shortest paths between other heroes. When two routes tie, each gets a fraction. A bypass can reduce that share without making a journey shorter.

What is measured, and what is shown?

The ripple view shows a disclosed sample of affected article pairs, with one possible shortest route per pair before and after. The brighter selected route is the journey named below the map. Metrics count all shortest paths, splitting credit across ties. All pairs are unordered and exclude the hero whose betweenness is being measured. Normalization is 2 / ((n − 1)(n − 2)). “Journeys shortened” counts all unordered pairs whose shortest distance falls. No characters are removed.

All 277 articles remain visible. The ripple view arranges stations schematically around the broker and bypass endpoints; it does not encode geographic or graph distance. Grey tracks are original links. Colored tracks trace the sampled shortest routes; their width represents how many displayed routes use that track. Full network uses a force layout. Positions stay fixed while you scrub. The three featured nodes are enlarged for legibility, and their ring area changes in proportion to their own before/after betweenness. Intermediate frames interpolate visually; they are not fractional graphs.

In the full network, drag between two stations to choose a link, or enable “Draw on map” and click two stations. The selectors provide the same actions with a keyboard. The moving marks illustrate routes, not measured traffic or a random sample of travelers. The drawing is inspired by comic printing and the visual language of Into the Spider-Verse.

Source: Week 3: Who matters, and why; course snapshot dated 26 August 2026, supplied in this repository. This is an experiment about article-link structure, not fictional power, observed traffic, or causation in social life.
