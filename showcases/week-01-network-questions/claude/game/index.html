AFTER THE SNAP

How the game uses Wikipedia

Each mission contains 24 articles and only real links from the frozen Marvel Wikipedia graph. For this game, link direction is ignored: a group is connected if its articles have paths between them. Ten announced targets are attacked; your three shields save three, leaving 17 survivors. Score = the largest connected group of survivors. The attack and shields are fictional rules, not Marvel relationships. The best defence checks all 120 possible three-shield choices. Retries use the same targets.
