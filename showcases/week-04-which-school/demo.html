Why the results differ

Louvain searches for groups with more internal links than expected under a degree-based null model. Its random visit order can lead to different partitions with similar modularity. Switching groups is evidence of instability across runs, not proof that a philosopher belongs to overlapping schools.

This demo replays 50 precomputed seeds in order. Labels are aligned to a reference partition so you can follow groups between runs. The graph has 1,374 philosophers and 9,139 undirected links; Louvain treats each link equally.
