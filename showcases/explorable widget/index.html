Word order is the information that disappeared. The sentences are different to a human reader, but Bag-of-Words sees five words appearing once each in every sentence.

Why this matters

A representation is a choice.

Bag-of-Words is useful because it makes text countable. It is limited because it keeps word identity and frequency while throwing away order. The same representation can be exactly right for one research question and useless for another.
