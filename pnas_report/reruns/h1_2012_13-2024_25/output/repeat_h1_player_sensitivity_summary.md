# Repeated H1 one-transfer-per-player sensitivity check

The restricted 2012/13–2024/25 input and Premier League lookup matched the reduction
manifest hashes. The sample contained 16,636 eligible transfers from 4,602 players.
The original selection (seed 2026) reproduced the saved result exactly,
including all 999 ordered shuffle gaps. Premier League entry rates were
19.52% at age 30+ (57/292)
and 21.46% under 30 (925/4310).
The original shuffle mean was 0.4582 percentage points.

| Statistic | Median | Central 95% | Original draw | Draws at or below original |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Observed gap (pp) | -3.1070 | [-5.7202, -0.6079] | -1.9412 | 813/999 (81.38%) |
| Observed gap minus its shuffle mean (pp) | -1.9948 | [-4.1198, 0.1609] | -2.3994 | 347/999 (34.73%) |
| Conditional lower-tail p-value | 0.119 | [0.004, 0.597] | 0.074 | 354/999 (35.44%) |

The central 95% uses the empirical 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles with linear interpolation.
The observed gap was below zero in 987/999 draws (98.80%)
and below its own shuffle mean in 963/999 draws (96.40%).
The spread measures sensitivity to which eligible transfer represents each player
in this fixed set of players. Each p-value is conditional on its selected transfers.

Each draw selected one transfer uniformly per player, with age classified at that
transfer. The selected transfers stayed fixed for 999 age-label permutations
within origin club and recorded season. Lower-tail p-values counted shuffled gaps
at or below the observed older-minus-younger gap, with a plus-one adjustment.
All draws contained 4,602 distinct players and both age groups. The original
permutation routine checked the age-group counts in every stratum of every shuffle.

Run from the repository root with Python 3.12.7 and NumPy 1.26.4.
NumPy `SeedSequence(20261126)` spawned 999 child seeds in draw order, and each child
spawned separate `default_rng` streams for transfer selection and shuffling.
The summary was generated by reading back the saved [per-draw CSV](repeat_h1_player_sensitivity_draws.csv).

```powershell
python -B pnas_report/reruns/h1_2012_13-2024_25/notebooks/repeat_h1_player_sensitivity.py
```
