# Louvain transfer communities

How much directed, weighted modularity does Louvain find in the eligible transfer network and in one randomized realization? The joining-rate analysis supplied 16,636 eligible records from 4,602 players. The network keeps all eligible destinations, including clubs outside the Premier League. The graph has 1,157 clubs and 11,016 directed routes, with one positive transfer-count weight per route. Club names follow the notebook's destination-over-origin convention. Conflicting names are listed in the results. Representatives maximize original in-degree plus out-degree, with ties resolved by club ID. This measure counts routes. Transfer strengths sum their weights.

Louvain starts from singletons and optimizes directed weighted modularity at gamma = 1. The optimizer seed is 2026. The NetworkX-based instrumented run records candidate evaluations and accepted moves, including supernode relocations. NetworkX independently scored every saved accepted state with absolute tolerance 1e-10. See the [results](louvain_results.json) for settings and versions. [Blondel et al. (2008)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-5468/2008/10/P10008), [Dugué and Perez (2015)](https://hal.science/hal-01231784) and [NetworkX Louvain API](https://networkx.org/documentation/networkx-3.6.1/reference/algorithms/generated/networkx.algorithms.community.louvain.louvain_communities.html).
