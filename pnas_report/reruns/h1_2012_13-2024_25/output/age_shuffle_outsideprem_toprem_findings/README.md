# Age-label permutation figures: restricted period

Recorded seasons 2012/13–2024/25. Same plotting functions, sizes, bins, fonts, colors and normal-fit conventions as the original producer. Standalone views are 3.58 × 3.4 inches; comparison is 7.16 × 3.4 inches; PNGs use 600 dpi.

**All eligible transfers.** Brown bars show 999 age-label permutations within origin club and recorded season. The orange line marks the observed gap (-2.42 percentage points); the blue line marks the null mean (-1.31 ± 0.83, one sample standard deviation). The brown normal fit is illustrative; inference uses the empirical permutations.

**One transfer per player.** Brown bars show 999 age-label permutations within origin club and recorded season. The orange line marks the observed gap (-1.94 percentage points); the blue line marks the null mean (0.46 ± 1.57, one sample standard deviation). The brown normal fit is illustrative; inference uses the empirical permutations.

**Combined comparison.** Panels (a) and (b) show all transfers and one fixed uniformly drawn transfer per player. Negative gaps mean lower older-group joining rates. Each panel retains its own 30-bin range. Legends reserve space above the data.
