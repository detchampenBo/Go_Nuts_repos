# Restricted feeder-route ownership screen

Screened on 21 November 2026. The review covered the 28 selected routes outside the reused audit snapshot and all 68 associated records. Some keys were mentioned in the earlier 334-route narrative screen and were reviewed again here. Event dates come from the saved selected-event table. Multiple records on the same day count separately. The model uses recorded seasons 2012/13–2024/25 and includes eligible events dated 1 July 2012–15 July 2025. [Coverage ledger](ownership_coverage_ledger.csv) · [Selected events](../output/premier_league_feeder_ownership_events.csv)

The screen looked for named common direct or indirect club-equity investors, including disclosed minority stakes, during the model interval and at each selected event. It established no additional common-equity interval or event overlap. Smaller historical holdings and beneficial ownership behind private vehicles remain incompletely disclosed. The evidence below records the dated club records reviewed and specific gaps. [Original definition and source limits](../../../notes/premier_league_feeder_ownership_audit.md)
