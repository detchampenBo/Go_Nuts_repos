[Paper, §§5–6.2](https://gksmyth.github.io/pubs/PermPValuesPreprint.pdf) | **Methods, supporting.** Section 6.2 identifies `(b + 1)/(B + 1)` as the conservative upper-bound calculation for `B` random draws and `b` equally or more extreme values. | Jesse Hemerik and Jelle Goeman (2018 issue, online 2017), *TEST* 27, 811–825, DOI `10.1007/s11749-017-0571-1`. Full text. [Paper, §3.3 Theorem 2 and §3.4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6405018/#Sec10) | **Methods, main permutation reference.** Formalizes the identity-plus-random-permutations test with draws from a transformation group and a null invariant under those transformations. [Spearman, rank comparison](https://academic.oup.com/ije/article/39/5/1137/806468) · [Efron, §4 Eq. 4.3](https://people.stat.sc.edu/hoyen/pastteaching/stat704-2022/Notes/Efron1981.pdf) | **Methods, supporting if room.** Rank correlation measures agreement in ordering between the continuous scores and ordered labels. - **H1.** The observed statistic is the pooled entry percentage for 30+ minus under 30. The implementation holds the transfer records and destinations fixed and shuffles age labels within **origin club × recorded source season**, preserving the counts of each age group in each block. The 999 draws use seed 2026 and the inclusive lower tail. Strata with only one age group still contribute to the statistic, and the shuffle distribution can have a nonzero centre. The approved result is 12.14% versus 14.56%, a −2.42 pp gap against a −1.31 pp mean shuffled gap, with limited evidence against the stated null (p = 0.098). The local design assumes labels can be reassigned among records within each block under that null. Stable player traits, the progression of age over a career and repeated records within and across blocks can violate that invariance. The shuffle does not preserve career histories or establish a causal age disadvantage. [H1 code](../../notebooks/age_shuffle_outsideprem_toprem.py) · [Restricted saved results](../../reruns/h1_2012_13-2024_25/output/age_shuffle_outsideprem_toprem_results.json) · [Approved interpretation](../evidence_and_sources.md#agreed-paper-wording-and-resolution)
- **H1 fixed-player sensitivity.** A separate seeded stream samples one eligible transfer uniformly per player, fixes those 4,602 records, and applies the same origin-club-by-season rule with a separate shuffle stream. This study chose that sensitivity to give each player equal representation. It changes the age and event mix, removes duplicated player representation in that draw, and leaves within-block exchangeability an assumption. The approved rates are 19.52% versus 21.46%, a −1.94 pp gap against a +0.46 pp mean shuffled gap (p = 0.074). One draw gives no measure of variation over other draws. Winkler et al. motivate the dependence concern, while the project note and saved verification support this particular design and result. [Sampling note](../../notes/one_transfer_per_player_sampling_methodology.md) · [Restricted verification](../../reruns/h1_2012_13-2024_25/provenance/h1_verification.json)
- **H2.** Average sentences within each signing-period, take after minus before, and compare the two age-group means with equal weight per signing. The code shuffles age labels among signings within **date-assigned transfer season only**. Use 5,000 draws, seed 2805, and the inclusive two-sided absolute-gap tail. The observed gap is +0.0060 RoBERTa score units (p = 0.7558), with limited sensitivity to smaller differences. Repeat signings for the same player and articles shared by different players can link signing outcomes. The shuffle preserves season age counts and relies on within-season exchangeability under the null. It gives no assignment of age or timing and supports no causal signing claim. Distinct within-age tests shuffle before/after labels in whole article blocks **within signing**, and their intervals bootstrap signing-level changes. An age group’s own period test does not answer the between-group question. The saved main H2 table exports no between-age interval and the historical interval stays out.
