How does player age relate to Premier League entry and Guardian coverage around signings? We modelled 16,636 eligible transfers from recorded seasons 2012/13--2024/25 as a directed club network. Transfers involving players aged 30+ entered the Premier League (PL) less often than those involving younger players (12.14\% versus 14.56\%). Evidence against a null model that shuffled age labels within origin club and season was limited (one-sided $p=0.098$). In our text corpus consisting of a partly common cohort of PL players, older players received more coverage before and after signing. To compare changes in tone, we retained 820 signings with sufficient text in before and after a transfer. Of four sentiment models checked against 196 human-labelled sentences, RoBERTa had the strongest rank agreement ($\rho=0.52$). The older-minus-younger gap in tone change was $+0.006$ score units (two-sided $p=0.76$), with no clear evidence that tone changed differently by age.
