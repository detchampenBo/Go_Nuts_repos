## Methods and citations

- Explain what a method measures, why it fits the question, and how it was applied here.
- Cite verified original contributions and the relevant implementation or adaptation.
- Refer readers to those sources for technical derivations.
- Retain the study-specific choices needed for reproduction, including data selection, preprocessing, controls, parameters, and validation.
- Attribute claims and theory to sources that directly support them.

## Interpretation

- Describe observational results through associations and comparisons.
- State the limitations of sentiment scores, sample selection, and null models where they affect interpretation.
- Explain how the evidence bears on the question, including findings with substantial uncertainty.
- Keep terminology consistent across the question, figures, results, and discussion.
