\subsection{Louvain transfer backbone}
\label{sec:louvain-transfer-backbone}

We applied the directed disparity filter \cite{serrano2009multiscale} to the
observed transfer-count-weighted network and to the induced network of each
saved Louvain community. A route survived when its source-outgoing or
destination-incoming upper-tail probability was strictly below the local
cutoff. We also checked the directed connectivity exception, which applied to
zero observed routes.

At $\alpha=0.20$, the whole-network backbone retained 1,684 of 12,794 routes
and 6,526 of 19,712 recorded transfers (33.1\%). This included 372 of 5,211
between-community routes representing 1,187 transfers. All 427 active clubs
were in one weak component, covering 34.1\% of the original 1,254 clubs. In the
induced community views, Lille first split into multiple active weak components
as the cutoff decreased, at $\alpha=0.41$. At $\alpha=0.10$, Wolves had nine
active weak components and the largest held 10 of its 180 clubs. Weak
connectivity ignores route direction. The cutoff describes locally
concentrated transfer counts under the disparity null.
