# Feeder-route shared-ownership research: other Premier League destinations

Research date: 2026-11-11. This is a bounded primary-source portfolio screen, prepared as one component of the 334-route ownership audit.

## Scope and interpretation

This file covers **200 directed origin/destination pairs into 29 destinations**. It excludes Manchester City [281], Chelsea [631], Crystal Palace [873], Brighton [1237], Watford [1010], Nottingham Forest [703], Leicester [1003], Brentford [1148], Arsenal [11], Southampton [180] and Wolves [543], researched separately.

The user selected the definition **any documented common equity owner, including minority interests**. A common sponsor, loan partnership, agent, director, nationality or family relationship does not by itself establish common equity. A documented stake in a football holding company can establish indirect club ownership; no assumption is made that passive investors in every remotely related public corporation are club owners.

The model retains recorded seasons 00/01–26/27. This screen distinguishes ownership overlap anywhere in that interval from overlap on an actual selected Premier League-arrival date. Future-dated records are not prospective evidence of future ownership. All event dates below were obtained by rebuilding the eligible PL-arrival sample with `notebooks/premier_league_feeder_routes.py::_build_sample` and only then joining the significant route keys. Joining the keys directly to all eligible-transfer rows would incorrectly include events when the destination was outside the PL.

**Result in this scope: one confirmed common-equity route, FC Lorient [1158] → AFC Bournemouth [989].** It has three ownership-concurrent selected records out of four. For the remaining 199 pairs, this initial screen found no documented common-equity match. The subsequent [historical-investor audit](feeder_ownership_definition_audit.md) adds Hearts → Liverpool as an interval-ever pair, with no transfer-concurrent record, bringing the combined result for this 200-route scope to two interval-ever pairs and one transfer-concurrent pair. This is a research-screen result, **not proof that every historical, indirect or small minority ownership link was absent**. Public club disclosures often enumerate only significant shareholders. Historical minority reconstruction remains incomplete; an exact all-investor negative census cannot be claimed from this work.

## Confirmed positive: Lorient ? Bournemouth

Bournemouth's [Companies House PSC record](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/06632170/persons-with-significant-control) identifies Black Knight Football Club UK Limited from 12 December 2022. Cannae's January 2023 announcement describes a **signed agreement** for the Lorient investment; Lorient's own announcement says minority shareholding would follow in the coming weeks. These announcement dates must not be substituted for the equity closing date. [Cannae announcement, 13 January 2023](https://www.cannaeholdings.com/news-releases/news-release-details/black-knight-football-club-led-managing-general-partner-william), [Lorient announcement](https://www.fclorient.bzh/communique-officiel-du-fc-lorient-4/).

Cannae's filed annual report explicitly dates completion of the Lorient minority acquisition to **1 February 2023**. Therefore the common-equity interval begins **2023-02-01**, not 2023-01-13. Bournemouth's 2024 accounts identify Lorient as 40%-owned by the immediate parent; Cannae and Lorient announced acquisition of the remaining 60% on 28 January 2026. The evidence supports continuing common equity to the research date; it does not establish ownership after that date. [SEC annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1704720/000170472023000025/R10.htm), [Bournemouth 2024 accounts](https://images.gc.afcbournemouthservices.co.uk/4213e360-089e-11f0-96ca-3b5257f7e8db.pdf), [Lorient 2026 announcement](https://www.fclorient.bzh/black-knight-football-club-devient-lactionnaire-unique-du-fc-lorient/).

## Salzburg ? Leeds: do not count a branded network as proven common equity

Leeds explicitly announced Red Bull's minority equity investment on **30 May 2024**. Its three selected Salzburg arrivals occurred on 1 July 2022 (Brenden Aaronson and Rasmus Kristensen) and 3 January 2023 (Maximilian Wober), all before that investment. [Leeds announcement](https://www.leedsunited.com/en/news/leeds-united-announce-red-bull-as-front-of-shirt-partner).

There is a second reason to avoid simply counting this pair as shared equity even for the broader modelling interval. The [Austrian official company register](https://www.evi.gv.at/f/452749h) identifies the association FC Red Bull Salzburg (ZVR 909741108) as the **sole shareholder of FC Red Bull Salzburg GmbH**, registered on 12 May 2016. The Austrian Bundesliga describes Salzburg as formally independent since 2015, with Red Bull as main sponsor. UEFA's June 2017 decision likewise describes the association as wholly owning the operating company and, after governance changes, found no decisive common influence over Salzburg and Leipzig. These facts do not negate sporting/network connections, but the evidence does not establish a Red Bull GmbH equity stake in Salzburg concurrent with its Leeds equity. [Austrian Bundesliga](https://www.bundesliga.at/de/news/artikel/salzburg-freut-sich-auf-spezielles-el-duell-mit-rb-leipzig), [UEFA decision](https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/0258-0e2dece33fb8-5cc21edafedf-1000/rb_leipzig_fc_salzburg_-_cfcb_adjudicatory_chamber_decision_-_june_2017.pdf).

Classify this route as **Red Bull-branded/network affiliation; common equity not established**, with **zero selected arrivals after the Leeds investment**. Do not add it to the strict common-equity total solely because of the Salzburg name.

## Reuse in the combined audit

Use the one confirmed positive to augment the independently sourced City/Chelsea/Palace/Brighton/Watford/Forest/Leicester/Brentford/Arsenal/Southampton/Wolves findings. Keep an explicit status such as `no_documented_link_found` or `not_fully_resolved` for all screened non-matches; do not encode them as logically proven absence of every common equity interest. If reporting a percentage out of 334, describe it as the **confirmed/documented proportion**, subject to the stated source coverage.

This work does not test whether shared ownership caused route concentration, whether transfers were permanent sales, or whether sell-on payments/profit arose. Those require separate transfer-type and financial evidence. The 334 routes were selected at unadjusted p <= 0.05 with 999 permutations; ?significant? retains that per-route meaning.
