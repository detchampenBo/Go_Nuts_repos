# Historical ownership evidence for feeder-route candidates

Research checked 11 November 2026. This note supports the ownership audit of the 334 routes with unadjusted Monte Carlo p <= 0.05 in [the filtered route table](../output/premier_league_feeder_significant_routes.csv). It covers candidate routes into Chelsea, Crystal Palace, Brighton, Watford, Nottingham Forest, Leicester, Brentford and Arsenal, plus related caveats for Wolves and Southampton. It is an evidence note, not a complete register of every shareholder in all 334 pairs.

## Definitions and timing

Count shared direct or indirect equity ownership, including minority ownership. A common disclosed ownership group or an explicit statement of common control in audited club accounts is stronger evidence than family relationships, agency relationships, loans, sponsorship, or cooperation alone. Exact corporate chains have not been independently reconstructed in every jurisdiction. Control and economic ownership must be distinguished when shares are placed in a trust.

Two questions give different counts: (1) did the clubs share ownership at any point in the modelling interval, and (2) did at least one of the selected route's actual recorded transfers occur during that shared-ownership period? Granada-Watford qualifies for the first but not the second. Open-ended dates below mean that no disposal terminating the documented connection was identified; they do not guarantee that ownership never changed after the most recent source.

The local transfer-date checks below apply the original season-specific GB1 destination and non-PL origin filters. Simply matching route IDs to every row of the eligible-transfer file incorrectly includes additional transfers when the destination was outside the PL. Each of the ten route totals below matches its count in the analysis.

## Primary evidence by candidate

### Strasbourg and Chelsea

The club's 22 June 2023 press release announces an agreement with the consortium that acquired Chelsea in May 2022. This is a first-party authored press release reproduced by a newspaper, not the newspaper's own ownership claim: [RCSA press release, 22 June 2023](https://cdn-s-www.dna.fr/pdf/fabdc231-1a78-4f0a-9c91-c6ba1a8b61c1/document.pdf). BlueCo's filed 2023 group accounts report a July 2023 closing: [BlueCo 22 Limited filing history, 6 March 2024 accounts filing](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/13949552/filing-history). The original Companies House document redirect was intermittently inaccessible, but the indexed accounts excerpt supplies the month; retain month precision.

Chelsea's own [30 May 2022 completion statement](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/statement-from-board-of-directors-of-chelsea-football-club-limit) establishes its transition from Abramovich to the Boehly/Clearlake-led group. Its [16 September 2026 ownership-transition announcement](https://www.chelseafc.com/en/news/article/chelsea-fc-announces-ownership-transition?ssoc=1) describes Clearlake acquiring other investors' interests. That announcement alone does not establish the transaction's legal completion, and it does not evidence a disposal of the continuing Clearlake interest.

### RWDM and Crystal Palace

The Palace investment announcement is dated [11 August 2021](https://www.cpfc.co.uk/news/announcement/john-textor-joins-crystal-palace-football-club-board-of-directors/). Textor's [own historical RWDM press archive](https://www.johntextor.org/press-molenbeek) records the January 2022 investment, whereas Eagle's [portfolio page](https://www.eaglefootball.com/home) says RWDM operational control was assumed in January 2023. Both precede the route's selected events. Palace's [24 July 2025 completion announcement](https://www.cpfc.co.uk/news/announcement/crystal-palace-welcomes-woody-johnson/) supplies the exit boundary.

The [CAS award, Crystal Palace v UEFA/Forest/Lyon](https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/02a0-1f5798b32beb-ab106cd55b7b-1000/cas_2025.a.11604_crystal_palace_football_club_v._uefa_nottingham_forest_football_club_olympique_lyonnais.pdf) also discusses the Palace-Molenbeek loans and Textor's controlling interest in Molenbeek, independently supporting the historical connection. A portfolio page still listing Palace after the sale is stale evidence and must not override the completed-sale announcement.

### Union SG and Brighton

Union's [7 July 2023 statement by Alex Muzio](https://rusg.brussels/en/news/statement-president-and-owner-alex-muzio-0) explicitly identifies Bloom as Brighton's majority owner, discusses his Union investment, dates the project to 2018, and states that Bloom has become a minority shareholder as Muzio takes the majority. Brighton's [official Tony Bloom biography](https://www.brightonandhovealbion.com/media-article/tony-bloom-mbe) dates his initial shareholder investment in Brighton to 2000 and chairmanship to May 2009. The Union change follows all six selected events.

### Udinese, Watford and Granada

The critical evidence for Udinese-Watford is accounting common control, not the Pozzo surname: Watford's [audited 2025 accounts](https://images.gc.watfordfcservices.co.uk/ae753480-2dc5-11f1-bc79-416e9c4833f1.pdf), printed page 36/PDF page 39, identify Udinese as a company under common control and identify Gino Pozzo as Watford's sole ultimate controlling party. The [2021 accounts on Watford's official document host](https://images.gc.watfordfcservices.co.uk/d30fc840-2d82-11ef-a0be-37a5b1f697a8.pdf) also contain related-party and ultimate-control disclosures. Udinese's [official 2017 sporting-director interview](https://www.udinese.it/news/squadra/gerolin-lavoreremo-sodo-per-tornare-ad-alti-livelli) explicitly describes Udinese and Watford as having the same ownership, and its [2026 ownership retrospective](https://www.udinese.it/news/magazine/udinese-and-the-pozzo-family-celebrate-40-years-together) dates the Udinese ownership to 28 July 1986 and includes Gino among the owning family.

The exact June 2012 Watford acquisition day was not recovered from a readable primary document in this sub-audit. Consequently, a date-coded appendix should distinguish the widely reported 2012 takeover date from the primary evidence verifying common control later; no exact day should be presented as newly proved here.

Granada's [official ownership-exit announcement](https://www.granadacf.es/noticias/jiang-lizhang-nuevo-maximo-accionista-del-granada-cf) identifies Gino as seller, describes the Pozzo project as concluded, and dates its beginning to summer 2009. Its [contemporary club-published media digest](https://www.granadacf.es/noticias/la-llegada-de-jiang-lizhang-situa-a-granada-en-el-foco-nacional-e-internacional) reports Jiang's 98.13% interest and completion on 14 June. This is weaker on exact closing-day precision because the club is summarising press coverage; the first selected event is 30 June, safely after the reported exit. Continued loans or player-rights arrangements do not establish continuing Granada equity ownership.

### Olympiacos, Rio Ave and Nottingham Forest

Olympiacos's [official yearbook](https://www.olympiacos.org/sites/default/files/book_en.pdf) dates Marinakis's 70% share acquisition to August 2010. Forest's [official August 2025 retrospective](https://livepreview.nottinghamforest.co.uk/news/2025/august/29/mr-marinakis---a-proud-day-for-everyone-/) dates the beginning of his ownership to 2017. Olympiacos also publishes his [August 2026 remarks explicitly referring to ownership of Forest](https://www.olympiacos.org/en/2026/08/28/evangelos-marinakis-if-you-believe-anything-is-possible/).

Rio Ave's [20 November 2023 member-approval statement](https://rioavefc.pt/destaque/maioria-expressiva-dos-socios-aprova-a-sad/) identifies the investor as Marinakis and links him to Forest and Olympiacos, but authorises negotiations rather than completing the share sale. The [15 June 2024 completion announcement](https://rioavefc.pt/destaque/comunicado-rio-ave-futebol-clube-futebol-sad/) records RAH Sports Investments acquiring 80%; the [official shareholding page](https://rioavefc.pt/sad/governo-da-sociedade/) lists RAH 80% and Rio Ave 20%.

Forest's parent-company [Companies House PSC register](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/10613412/persons-with-significant-control) shows Marinakis's registered control interrupted on 29 April 2025, restored on 6 June 2025, interrupted again on 17 April 2026, and restored on 10 June 2026. The register identifies Pittville One and Pittville Four during the gaps. A control change is not automatically a disposal of the underlying investor's beneficial economic interest. The register does not itself supply the complete trust deed or settle that economic question. The 2025 trust chronology also appears in the [CAS award hosted by UEFA](https://editorial.uefa.com/resources/02a0-1f5798b32beb-ab106cd55b7b-1000/cas_2025.a.11604_crystal_palace_football_club_v._uefa_nottingham_forest_football_club_olympique_lyonnais.pdf). Regardless, none of the ten selected Forest events above falls within either control gap, so the gaps do not change their event-overlap count.

### OH Leuven and Leicester

King Power's [12 June 2017 completion release](https://s3-ap-southeast-1.amazonaws.com/story.kingpower.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/13152814/Press_Statement_120617KP_OHL_Takeover_ENG.pdf) dates the concluded 92% acquisition to 6 June and identifies Leicester as already in the same portfolio. Leicester's [October 2017 statement](https://www.lcfc.com/media-article/King-Power-To-Unite-Fans-Of-Leicester-City-and-OH-Leuven) independently dates its King Power ownership to 2010 and OH Leuven's to June 2017. Leicester's [official 2025 accounts](https://downloads.ctfassets.net/0wcpt07qzvlh/4qxAe2QWQRSvsvnjeD5fpJ/7fe13c11806de9ec9c42da9613f1e95b/LCFC_Financial_Statements_30_June_2025_Signed.pdf) still disclose OH Leuven under the King Power related-party section. An OHL historical webpage gives a signing anniversary one day earlier, whereas the owner's contemporaneous completion release explicitly distinguishes completion; use 6 June for closing.

### Midtjylland and Brentford

Brentford's [2 July 2014 announcement](https://www.brentfordfc.com/en/news/matthew-benham-takes-majority-shareholding-in-fc-midtjylland_62788) simultaneously establishes Benham's existing Brentford ownership and new FCM majority. Its [28 February 2014 shareholding announcement](https://www.brentfordfc.com/en/news/benham-gains-full-brentford-shareholding_61689) records 96% ownership since June 2012 and the move to 100% in February 2014. FCM's [15 August 2023 ownership announcement](https://www.fcm.dk/nyheder/heartland-koeber-fc-midtjylland/) records HEARTLAND buying Benham's shares, with the incoming holding at 95.5%; it also notes regulatory conditions. FCM's later [official club history](https://www.fcm.dk/klubben/) confirms Benham's exit. Brentford's [July 2025 investment announcement](https://pre-prod.brentfordfc.com/en/news/club-news-brentford-new-investment-gary-lubner-sir-matthew-vaughn) says Benham remains its majority owner despite new minority investors. That later Brentford change does not restore his exited FCM stake.

### Colorado Rapids and Arsenal

Arsenal's [official board biography](https://www.arsenal.com/the-arsenal-board) dates Kroenke's first Arsenal shareholding to 2007 and full ownership to 2018, and supplies the Arsenal-KSE ownership chain. Colorado's [official owner biography](https://www.coloradorapids.com/club/stan-kroenke) explicitly identifies both clubs among KSE/Kroenke's football interests, and its [front-office disclosure](https://www.coloradorapids.com/club/front-office) identifies Stan Kroenke as owner/chairman. A [2007 City of Westminster lease memorandum](https://www.westminsterco.gov/Portals/1/Documents/Government%20-%20Documents/City%20Council/AgendaArchive/ag021207.pdf) records KSE taking over the Rapids lease as new owner in July 2004. Both selected movements concern the same player, Auston Trusty; two movement records must not be presented as two distinct players.

## Important exclusions and unresolved relationships

- **Grasshoppers-Wolves:** Grasshoppers' [2019/20 official yearbook](https://www.gcz.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/gcz.ch/club/Fans/Jahrbuch/GC_Jahrbuch_2019-20.pdf) identifies Jenny Wang/Champion Union as new owner from 9 April 2020. Wolves' [2016 takeover announcement](https://www.wolves.co.uk/news/club/2016072016-fosun-group-takes-over-wolves/) identifies Fosun/Guo Guangchang, not Jenny Wang, as purchaser. Grasshoppers announced [LAFC majority ownership on 17 January 2024](https://www.gcz.ch/news/artikel/27/information-betreffend-verkauf-der-aktien-der-grasshopper-fussball-ag/). A spouse or family relationship is insufficient to establish an identical equity owner. Keep this family-connected case separate unless a common beneficial holder or corporate-control chain is documented.
- **Vitesse-Chelsea:** do not label this confidently independent merely because the registered owners differ. The [2015 KNVB investigation](https://www.knvb.nl/nieuws/betaald-voetbal/licenties/15538/structuur-en-overname-vitesse-volgens-regels-knvb) reported no evidence of Chelsea control. However, the [19 April 2024 KNVB statement](https://www.knvb.nl/nieuws/betaald-voetbal/licentiezaken/69055/licentiecommisie-18-wedstrijdpunten-mindering-voor) subsequently reported indications Abramovich had or had held control, with a ministry investigation pending. [Vitesse's 2022/23 accounts](https://api-prod.vitesse.nl/.uc/fe4aeaa570102b79810005f7ab0021d010fb2a7c135cf00/verslag-2223-ondertekend.pdf) discuss alleged indirect funding during 2010-2016 and unknown historical arrangements. This audit did not establish a dated common-equity interval. Classify as unresolved indirect ownership/control evidence, excluded from the verified-positive count rather than proved negative.
- **Botafogo-Forest:** Eagle's [historical club portfolio](https://www.eaglefootball.com/clubs-1) places Botafogo with Textor/Eagle, whereas the cited Forest primary disclosures identify Marinakis. Frequent transactions alone do not establish a shared equity owner. No common investor was verified here.
- **Estoril-Wolves and Famalicao-Wolves:** shared agents, player-trading connections, or investment-company relationships should not be coded as the same owner without an equity link at club level. No such common club equity owner was verified in this sub-audit.
- **Southampton candidates:** Southampton's [official ownership/board disclosure](https://www.southamptonfc.com/en/meet-the-saints-leadership-team) identifies Sport Republic and Katharina Liebherr, and identifies Goztepe and Valenciennes in Sport Republic's football portfolio. Neither is among Southampton's significant supplying origins in this sample. Its [official history](https://www.southamptonfc.com/en/history) records the Gao ownership interval from August 2017 to January 2022. No verified common owner with the significant supplying clubs was identified; this is not proof that no historic minority connection ever existed.

The remaining supplying origins for the assigned destination clubs were screened against the documented portfolios above. Lack of a found link is recorded as no shared owner identified, not as an exhaustive negative proof. Private minority holdings, trusts, and historic ownership chains can be incompletely public.
